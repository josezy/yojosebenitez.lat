Pleno – Answer Sheet Scanning App

Grading paper tests from a phone camera, fully on-device

  • Client: Santillana via Somos Gente Digital
  • Role: Lead mobile engineer
  • App Store
  • Google Play

Overview

Pleno is Santillana's assessment app for teachers. A teacher points the phone at a stack of printed answer sheets, and the app auto-captures each one, identifies the student and evaluation from its QR code, reads the marked answers, and syncs graded results to the Pleno platform — replacing manual grading of written tests.

  • Problem

    Reliable OMR grading from a phone camera in real classrooms

  • Stage

    In production on iOS and Android (Chile and Brazil)

  • Stack

    React Native · Expo · OpenCV · TypeScript · SQLite · AWS S3

Constraints

  • Sheets are photographed on dark desks, under uneven classroom lighting, at an angle, and sometimes printed in halftone on low-quality printers.
  • Everything must run on mid- and low-end Android phones, not just recent iPhones.
  • School networks are unreliable: no scan can be lost if the upload fails or the app is killed mid-session.
  • One codebase serves two brands and countries — Pleno (Chile) and Moderna Core (Brazil) — with different sheet formats, features, themes, and languages.

Pipeline

Auto-capture

  • Camera frames sampled at ~3 FPS; QR detection runs on a cropped region of interest.
  • Tilt, skew, and coverage metrics computed from the QR corners drive live framing feedback; a stability counter triggers capture only once the sheet is held steady.
  • The QR payload identifies the sheet type, evaluation, and student.

Geometry

  • Search zones for the corner markers are projected per sheet layout (80-question, 20-question, and geometric sheets).
  • Marker detection hardened against dark tables, halftone print, and strong perspective, with a fallback cascade from detected to interpolated corners.
  • OpenCV perspective warp to a normalized sheet before reading answers.

Grading

  • Illumination flattening that preserves paper tone, then z-score bubble classification.
  • Geometric sheets read via ridge detection and per-cell ink measurement.
  • Multiple-choice scoring against the answer key, with an overlay preview and editing of processed sheets before submitting.

Key decisions

Offline-first scan sessions

Every captured sheet is persisted to SQLite and the file system before upload. A retry queue survives app kills, network banners let teachers resume pending work, and partial server failures are reported per sheet instead of failing the whole batch.

Tenants as configuration

A typed tenant matrix gates modules per brand and country, with per-tenant themes, i18n (es-419, es-CL, pt-BR), and separate dev / pre / pro binaries generated through Expo prebuild (CNG) instead of hand-maintained native projects.

Golden tests for computer vision

A golden OCR harness with real warped sheet fixtures runs the detector end to end, so changes to marker detection or classification thresholds are checked against known answers before reaching teachers.

Security and permission UX

OAuth tokens moved from AsyncStorage to SecureStore, login uses ephemeral browser sessions, and the iOS tracking prompt is deferred until after the first successful scan — so the first-run experience starts with the camera, not with permission dialogs.

Outcome

  • Shipped 15+ store builds across iOS and Android, from 1.19 through 1.21, with 2.0 in progress.
  • Extended the app from Chile to Brazil (Moderna Core) from a single codebase.
  • Three sheet formats read on-device, plus mock-exam batches where each sheet's QR resolves its class, subject, evaluation, and student.
  • Added nominative answer-sheet generation, OCR student identification with suggestions, PDF preview, and English / Portuguese sheets.
